{# --- Target size --- #}

{% trans "Target size" %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} Thumbs, tremors and cracked screens all miss small targets. Buttons, links and inputs need room to hit. Text inputs also need text of at least 16px: below that, iPhones zoom in when you tap the field, and the page jumps. {% endblocktrans %}

WCAG 2.5.8 (AA, 24px), 2.5.5 (AAA, 44px)

{# --- Keyboard --- #}

{% trans "Keyboard navigation" %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} Some people can't use a mouse: they move with Tab and act with Enter or Space. They need to see where they are, and to move in the order they read. Click just above a row, then press Tab. {% endblocktrans %}

WCAG 2.1.1 (A), 2.4.3 (A), 2.4.7 (AA), 2.4.11 (AA)

{# --- Skip link --- #}

{% trans "Skip to main content" %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} Without a skip link, a keyboard user tabs through every link in the header on every page before reaching what they came for. Every page on the portal now starts with one. Reload this page and press Tab once: "Skip to main content" appears in the top corner, and Enter jumps straight past the header. {% endblocktrans %}

WCAG 2.4.1 (A)

{# --- Forms --- #}

{% trans "Form labels and errors" %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} A placeholder isn't a label: it disappears as soon as you type, it's often too faint to read, and screen readers may skip it. An error has to say what went wrong and how to fix it, next to the field, in words, not only a red border. {% endblocktrans %}

WCAG 1.3.1 (A), 3.3.1 (A), 3.3.2 (A)
