# Managing users: a guide for court admins

This guide is for the court admin account on your court's Litigant Portal. It explains how to give your team access and how to help someone who can't sign in.

To start, sign in and open **Admin** in the menu. You will see the **Users** tab.

## Before you begin

While the portal is in testing, everyone needs the **site password** before they see the sign-in page. Share it with each person you add, along with their invite. Your Free Law Project contact can give it to you.

## Add a person

1. On the Users tab, choose **Add account**.
2. Enter their email address and choose **Add account**.
3. They get an email with a link to set their password. Until they do, the list shows **Invited** next to their address.

The link works for 7 days. After they set a password, they sign in with their email address and that password.

If the page says the invite was not sent, the account was still added. Use **Send password link** to try again.

## Someone can't sign in

- **They lost the invite, or the link expired:** choose **Send password link** next to their address. They get a new invite.
- **They forgot their password:** they can choose **Forgot your password?** on the sign-in page. You can also choose **Send password link** for them. Their old password keeps working until they set a new one.

- **They made their own account and can't sign in:** a person who signs up on their own must confirm their email address first. They get an email with a link, which works for 7 days. If they lost it, they can sign in again and a new email is sent. This can take a few minutes, and it may land in the spam folder. People you add yourself skip this step.

You never see or choose anyone's password.

## Remove access

- **Disable** stops the person from signing in. Everything they saved stays, and you can choose **Enable** later to let them back in.
- **Delete** removes the account and everything it saved: chats, uploads, and answers. This can't be undone.

## What you can't do

You can manage the people you add, but not other court admins or your own account. To change your own password, use **Change your own password** at the top of the Users tab. If you forget your password, use **Forgot your password?** on the sign-in page.

## Instances without email

Some instances can't send email yet. There, **Add account** asks you to type a password, and the button next to each person is **Password** instead of **Send password link**. Share the password with the person directly and safely, never by plain email or chat. People who sign up on their own are not asked to confirm their email address there.
