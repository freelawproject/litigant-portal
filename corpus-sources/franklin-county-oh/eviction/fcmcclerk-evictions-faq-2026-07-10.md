# FCMC Clerk — Evictions FAQ

Source: https://www.fcmcclerk.com/faq/civil/evictions
Captured: 2026-07-10 (WebFetch extraction; near-verbatim Q&A)

**1. What are the circumstances surrounding the eviction of a person?**
"That information is contained in the complaint, which is maintained in a case file in our fileroom."

**2. Am I being evicted?**
"Look up your name on our public access case search to see if a case has been filed."

**3. What are my rights as a tenant?**
"You can call the Columbus Urban League at (614) 257-6300 for information."

**4. How much does it cost to file an eviction case?**
- Single cause (eviction only): $128.00
- Two causes (eviction + money request): $165.00

Fees cover ordinary mail service plus one service method for up to 3 defendants/addresses. Additional defendants incur extra charges per court fee schedule. Service options include: Bailiff Service or Certified Mail (signature required).

**5. How do I file an Eviction, and where do I get the complaint forms?**
Refer to the Eviction Process PDF document for filing guidance and complaint forms.

**6. How do I red tag the door and do a set out?**
After filing, the Clerk's office sends paperwork to the service bailiff's office. Contact them at (614) 645-7780 to arrange the set out.

**7. Does the owner of the property need to file the eviction?**
"Please seek guidance from an attorney or the Self Help Center for assistance in these special matters."

**8. When will the defendant be served?**
Timing depends on postal service or bailiff. "Usually the hearings will be scheduled fourteen to twenty-one days from the filing date."

**Court contact:** (614) 645-8186 | 375 South High Street, Columbus, Ohio 43215
